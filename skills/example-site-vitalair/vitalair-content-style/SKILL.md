# VitalAir — Content & Writing Style

Write all VitalAir copy in the brand's established voice: **warm, local, plain-spoken, and trust-building**. VitalAir Services is an Atlanta-area HVAC company (AC repair, heating, installation, replacement, indoor air quality, ductless mini-splits). A local Daikin & Goodman dealer.

## Voice in one line

A trusted local expert who explains things clearly, treats customers like family, and never pressures or hypes.

## Core voice traits

- **Local and specific.** Name the service area often: Norcross, Gwinnett County, Canton, Marietta, Brookhaven, Sandy Springs, Woodstock, "the Greater Atlanta area." Location grounds every page.
- **Plain and clear.** Short, direct sentences. No jargon dumps, no hype ("world-class," "revolutionary" — avoid). Explain the "why" simply.
- **Reassuring, low-pressure.** Emphasize informed choice: "explains your repair options clearly, so you can make an informed decision." Help the reader decide; don't push.
- **Warm and personal.** "We treat every customer like family." Use "we/our" for VitalAir and "you/your" for the reader.
- **Benefit-focused.** Tie features to homeowner outcomes: comfort, efficiency, safety, dependable performance, lower bills.
- **Trust signals.** 24/7 service, highly trained technicians, proper sizing, clear explanations before repairs, clean workmanship, manufacturer dealer status.

## Formatting conventions

- **Eyebrow labels:** ALL CAPS, short (e.g. `AC REPAIR`, `COOLING SERVICES`, `DUCTLESS MINI-SPLIT SOLUTIONS`).
- **Headings:** Sentence case or Title Case, descriptive + location, e.g. "AC Repair in Norcross, GA and the Greater Atlanta Area", "Complete Cooling Services for Atlanta-Area Homes."
- **Sub-headings:** benefit phrases — "A Complete, Clean Installation", "Clear Explanations Before Repairs", "Accurate Room Evaluations."
- **Body:** 1–3 short sentences per paragraph. Second person.

## Reusable section patterns

**Hero intro (under the h1):**
> 24/7 AC repair, heating, and indoor air quality service for Norcross, Gwinnett County, and the Greater Atlanta area — repairs, installs, and maintenance from a local Daikin & Goodman dealer.

**Symptom → reassurance → CTA** (the signature service paragraph):
> If your AC is blowing warm air, making strange noises, leaking water, short cycling, or producing weak airflow, it may be time for professional AC repair. Vital Air Services diagnoses the issue and explains your repair options clearly, so you can make an informed decision.

**Closing CTA paragraph:**
> Do not wait for a small AC issue to become a major breakdown. Whether you need repair, maintenance, installation, or replacement, Vital Air Services is ready to help. Contact Vital Air Services today to schedule service in Norcross, Gwinnett County, and the Greater Atlanta area.

**"About" trust line:**
> At Vital Air Services, we treat every customer like family, and we want you to feel confident choosing us for all your Atlanta HVAC needs.

**FAQ intro:**
> A few of the questions homeowners ask us most. Don't see yours? We're happy to talk it through.

## CTA / button copy

Use the kit's established phrasings: "Contact Us Today", "Schedule Service", "Schedule a Service Inspection", "Schedule a Repair", "Book your appointment today!", "Get Quote", "Read More", "Learn more". Keep them short, active, and friendly.

## Decision-framing lists (repair vs. replace)

The kit uses honest, plain "makes sense if" lists. Mirror this pattern:
- "A professional repair makes sense if: …"
- "A complete system replacement makes sense if: …"

Frame it as helping the homeowner choose — never as an upsell.

## Do / Don't

**Do:** name the location; explain plainly; lead with homeowner benefits; reassure; end with a clear CTA; write short paragraphs.

**Don't:** use hype or superlatives; write long dense blocks; use heavy technical jargon without a plain explanation; sound pushy or salesy; drop the local/Atlanta framing.

## Company name usage

Full name **Vital Air Services** in body copy; **VitalAir** acceptable in labels/branding. Be consistent within a page.
