What we fix

Types of AC Problems We Repair

Our technicians inspect the full cooling system, find the cause, and explain the repair clearly before any work begins.

AC Blowing Warm Air

Warm air from a running AC can mean low refrigerant, a dirty coil, airflow restrictions, thermostat issues, or compressor trouble. We find out whether it is a simple fix, like a clogged filter, or something more serious, like a refrigerant leak.

Weak Airflow or Uneven Cooling

Rooms that stay warm while the AC runs often point to a dirty filter, blower motor problems, blocked vents, ductwork issues, or frozen coils. Repairing the cause improves air movement and reduces hot spots.

AC Not Turning On

A system that will not start may involve the thermostat, electrical connections, capacitor, contactor, circuit breaker, or outdoor condenser. We check each one so you are not left guessing.

Frequent Short Cycling

An AC that turns on and off too often uses more energy and wears faster. Common causes include thermostat issues, restricted airflow, low refrigerant, overheating parts, or an improperly sized system.

Frozen Coils or Refrigerant Lines

Ice on your AC is not normal during cooling season. It can mean restricted airflow, low refrigerant, dirty coils, or blower problems, and running a frozen system raises the risk of compressor damage.

Water Leaks Around the AC

Water around the indoor unit usually comes from a clogged condensate drain, a frozen coil, a damaged drain pan, or an installation issue. Fixing it early prevents water damage.

Loud Noises or Strange Smells

Banging, clanking, loud buzzing, or burning and musty odors can point to loose parts, electrical issues, motor problems, or moisture buildup. We find the source and recommend the safest repair.
