## Frequently Asked Questions
Here are some of the most common questions about germicidal lights. If you don’t see your question here, reach out, and we’ll be happy to give you an answer.
**Q: What are germicidal lights?**
A: Germicidal lights, often called UV-C lights, are devices that emit ultraviolet light to neutralize bacteria, viruses, and mold spores. They’re typically installed inside HVAC systems, so they treat the air as it circulates. This creates a healthier indoor environment for your home.
**Q: How do germicidal lights work in HVAC systems?**
A: These lights are placed near coils or within ducts where they shine on passing air. The UV light damages the DNA of microorganisms, stopping them from multiplying. As a result, germs and mold are neutralized before reaching your living spaces.
**Q: Are germicidal lights safe for my home?**
A: Yes, when installed by trained technicians, UV-C lights are completely safe. They’re contained within your HVAC system, so the light doesn’t reach your living space. The only thing you’ll notice is cleaner, fresher air.
**Q: How often do germicidal lights need maintenance?**
A: Most UV bulbs last about a year before they need to be replaced. We recommend adding the replacement to your routine HVAC maintenance schedule. Regular service keeps the system working at full strength.
**Q: Can germicidal lights help with allergies?**
A: Yes, by reducing mold spores and bacteria, germicidal lights can ease symptoms for those with allergies or asthma. Cleaner air makes a noticeable difference in comfort. Many homeowners report fewer flare-ups after installation.
**Q: Do germicidal lights eliminate all indoor air problems?**
A: While they’re highly effective against germs and mold, they don’t remove dust, pet dander, or chemical pollutants. For full indoor air quality improvement, they’re often combined with filters or air purifiers. Together, these systems offer complete protection.
**Q: Will UV lights affect my HVAC system?**
A: Germicidal lights actually help your system by keeping coils cleaner and reducing microbial buildup. This improves airflow and efficiency. Over time, it can also extend the lifespan of your equipment.
**Q: Can I install germicidal lights myself?**
A: Installation should be done by a trained technician because placement and safety are critical for effectiveness. DIY setups can lead to poor performance or even damage to your system. Professional installation guarantees the lights do their job properly.
**Q: How long does installation take?**
A: Most germicidal light installations can be completed in a single visit, often within a couple of hours. The exact time depends on your system’s layout. We’ll let you know what to expect before starting.
