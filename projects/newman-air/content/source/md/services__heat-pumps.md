## Frequently Asked Questions
Here are some common questions we hear about heat pumps. If you don’t see your question here, reach out and we’ll provide you with an answer.
**Q: How long do heat pumps usually last?**
A: Most heat pumps last 10 to 15 years with proper maintenance. Regular service can help extend their lifespan and keep performance strong. The type of system and how often it’s used also play a role.
**Q: How do I know if my heat pump needs a repair or replacement?**
A: Signs like frequent repairs, rising energy bills, or uneven comfort often point to bigger issues. If your system is older than 10 years, replacement may save you more money in the long run. A technician can inspect the unit and recommend the best option.
**Q: Are heat pumps energy efficient?**
A: Yes, heat pumps are one of the most efficient HVAC systems available because they transfer heat instead of generating it. This reduces energy use compared to traditional heating and cooling systems. Many homeowners see lower monthly utility bills after switching.
**Q: Can a heat pump work in cold climates?**
A: Modern heat pumps are designed to work well even in colder weather. In very low temperatures, some systems may need backup heating for extra support. We’ll help you choose the right unit for the climate.
**Q: What kind of maintenance does a heat pump need?**
A: Routine maintenance includes cleaning filters, checking refrigerant levels, and inspecting electrical components. We recommend at least one professional tune-up per year. Regular maintenance keeps your system efficient and prevents costly breakdowns.
**Q: How long does heat pump installation take?**
A: Installation usually takes one to two days, depending on the size of your system and the setup of your home. We’ll give you a clear timeline before we start. Our goal is to complete the work with minimal disruption to your schedule.
**Q: What are the benefits of replacing an older heat pump?**
A: Newer heat pumps are more efficient and reliable, often saving you money on energy bills. They also offer improved comfort and quieter operation. A new system is a long-term investment in your home’s comfort.
**Q: Can heat pumps improve indoor air quality?**
A: Yes, heat pumps circulate and filter air while heating or cooling, which can reduce dust and allergens. When paired with proper filters or indoor air quality systems, they can make your home’s air much cleaner. This is especially helpful for families with allergies or asthma.
**Q: Is financing available for your heat pump services?**
A: Yes, we offer financing through GoodLeap and Synchrony. These options allow you to spread out the cost of installation or major repairs. It’s an easy way to get the service you need without financial stress.
