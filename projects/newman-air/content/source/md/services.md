## Frequently Asked Questions
Here are some answers to common HVAC questions. If you don’t see your concern covered, reach out and we’ll provide you with the information you need.
**Q: How often should I schedule HVAC maintenance?**
A: Twice a year is recommended; once in spring for cooling and once in fall for heating. These visits keep your system prepared for seasonal demand. Regular maintenance lowers costs and extends lifespan.
**Q: Can I handle small HVAC repairs on my own?**
A: While you can change filters and check thermostats, most repairs need a trained technician. HVAC systems are complex and unsafe to handle without proper tools. Calling a pro protects both your home and your equipment.
**Q: What’s the benefit of installing a ductless system?**
A: Ductless systems provide zoned comfort without needing ductwork. They’re energy-efficient, quiet, and allow for room-by-room control. Many homeowners use them for additions or spaces with poor airflow.
**Q: How long does an HVAC system usually last?**
A: With regular maintenance, most systems last 12 to 15 years. Usage, climate, and upkeep all play a role in lifespan. When repairs become too frequent, replacement may be the best choice.
**Q: Why is my system making strange noises?**
A: Noises like banging, rattling, or hissing often signal loose parts, airflow problems, or failing components. Ignoring them can lead to bigger issues. A technician can diagnose and repair the cause.
**Q: What’s included in an air quality service?**
A: Air quality services may include air cleaners, humidifiers, dehumidifiers, and ventilation improvements. These solutions reduce allergens, pollutants, and excess moisture. The result is a healthier indoor environment.
**Q: Can a new thermostat lower my energy bills?**
A: Yes, upgrading to a programmable or smart thermostat gives you more control. These devices let you adjust settings automatically or remotely, saving energy when you’re not home. Over time, they often pay for themselves.
**Q: Do you offer services for businesses as well as homes?**
A: Yes, we provide HVAC installation, repair, and maintenance for commercial properties too. Businesses rely on consistent comfort, and our team has the expertise to keep systems running. Reliable service supports smooth operations.
**Q: How do I know when it’s time to replace my AC?**
A: Signs include rising energy bills, frequent repairs, or uneven cooling. If your unit is more than 10 to 15 years old, replacement often makes more financial sense. A new system provides better comfort and efficiency.
