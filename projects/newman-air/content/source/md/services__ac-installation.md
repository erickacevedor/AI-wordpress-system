## Signs It May Be Time for a New AC
Air conditioners rarely fail all at once without warning. Most systems show signs of decline before they stop working completely. Paying attention to those signs can help you avoid an emergency replacement during the hottest part of the year.
It may be time to consider a new AC system if you notice:
- Warm air blowing from the vents while the AC is running
- Uneven cooling or hot and cold spots throughout your home
- Frequent AC repairs or repeat service calls
- Rising energy bills without a clear explanation
- Grinding, rattling, buzzing, or other unusual noises
- A system that runs constantly but still cannot keep up
- Excess indoor humidity during the summer
- An AC unit that is more than 10 to 15 years old
If your system is newer and the issue is minor, repair may still be the better choice. If your AC is older, inefficient, and becoming expensive to keep running, replacement may provide better long-term value.

## What Happens During AC Installation?
Professional AC installation involves more than swapping out the outdoor unit. Best In Show follows a careful process to make sure your new system is properly selected, installed, tested, and ready to keep your Johnson City home comfortable.
### 1. We evaluate your current cooling system
Your technician starts by looking at your existing AC, indoor equipment, ductwork, thermostat, and overall comfort concerns. This helps identify whether replacement is the right move and whether any airflow, electrical, or installation issues need to be addressed.
### 2. We help you choose the right AC system
Next, we recommend cooling options based on your home’s size, layout, efficiency goals, and budget. Proper sizing matters because an undersized unit may run constantly, while an oversized unit may cool too quickly without removing enough humidity.
### 3. We remove the old equipment
Once the right system is selected, our team safely removes the old AC equipment and prepares the installation area. This step helps create a clean, secure setup for the new system.
### 4. We install and connect the new system
Your new AC is installed with care, including key connections, components, refrigerant lines, electrical connections, and thermostat setup as needed. The goal is to make sure the system is installed correctly from the start, not simply placed where the old unit used to be.
### 5. We test performance before we leave
After installation, we test the system to confirm it is cooling properly, operating safely, and moving air as expected. We also review basic system use, maintenance tips, and any next steps so you feel confident using your new AC.
## Why Waiting Too Long Can Cost More
It is understandable to delay AC replacement if your system is still running. But waiting too long can sometimes create bigger problems, especially during peak summer heat in Johnson City and the Tri-Cities.
An aging AC often has to work harder to produce less comfort. That can lead to higher utility bills, more frequent repairs, and a greater chance of a sudden breakdown when you need cooling most.
Delaying a needed installation can increase the risk of:
- Complete system failure during extreme heat
- Emergency repair costs
- Higher monthly energy bills
- Poor humidity control
- Uneven cooling from room to room
- Added strain on other HVAC components
- Fewer replacement options if your system fails unexpectedly
Replacing your AC before it completely breaks down gives you more time to compare options, ask questions, and choose the system that makes the most sense for your home.

## Frequently Asked Questions
Here are answers to common questions homeowners ask before scheduling AC installation in Johnson City, TN.
**Q: How long does an AC installation take?**
A: Most installations can be completed in one day, though larger or more complex systems may take longer. Our technicians will give you a clear timeline before we begin. We’ll also clean up the work area, so you don’t have to worry about the mess.
**Q: What size air conditioner do I need?**
A: The right size depends on your home’s square footage, insulation, layout, and other factors. Installing a unit that’s too small or too large can cause efficiency problems and uneven cooling. We’ll do a full assessment to match your home with the best system.
**Q: Can a new AC lower my energy bills?**
A: Yes, newer systems are designed to use less energy while delivering better performance. Many homeowners notice a reduction in their monthly bills after upgrading. The savings can be significant over the life of the system.
**Q: Do I need to replace my ductwork when installing a new AC?**
A: Not always, but old or damaged ductwork can limit the efficiency of a new unit. We’ll inspect your ducts during the installation process to see if repairs or replacements are needed. Proper airflow is key to getting the best results.
**Q: How do I know if my current AC needs to be replaced?**
A: If your unit is over 10 years old, needs frequent repairs, or struggles to keep your home comfortable, replacement may be the best option. Rising energy bills are also a strong indicator. Our team can evaluate your system and give you an honest recommendation.
**Q: Will I need regular maintenance on a new AC?**
A: Yes, regular maintenance keeps your system running at peak efficiency and extends its lifespan. Simple tasks like filter changes, plus annual professional tune-ups, are important. We offer maintenance options to make this easy for you.
**Q: What brands of air conditioners do you install?**
A: We work with top brands known for quality, efficiency, and reliability. Our technicians will explain your options so you can choose the brand and model that best fits your needs. Every installation is handled with care and precision.
**Q: Can I finance the installation of a new AC?**
A: Yes, we provide financing options through GoodLeap and Synchrony. This allows you to spread out the cost of your system over time. Many homeowners find this makes upgrading more accessible and stress-free.
**Q: How long will a new AC last?**
A: With proper maintenance, most modern air conditioners last between 12 and 15 years. Some units may last even longer, depending on usage and care. Investing in regular servicing helps maximize the lifespan of your system.
**Q: Will a new AC help with indoor humidity?**
A: It can, especially if your current system is oversized, aging, or not running properly. A correctly sized AC helps remove moisture from the air as it cools your home. This is especially important during humid Tri-Cities summers.
**Q: What brands of air conditioners do you install?**
A: Best In Show works with quality cooling equipment designed for dependable performance, efficiency, and comfort. Your technician can explain available options and help you choose a system that fits your home, preferences, and budget.
