## Frequently Asked Questions
Here are answers to common questions we hear from homeowners in Greeneville. If you don’t see your question, get in touch and we’ll provide you with an answer.
**Q: How often should my HVAC system be serviced?**
A: We recommend having maintenance done once in the spring and again in the fall. This keeps your system ready for the heavy demands of summer and winter. Routine care helps reduce energy costs and avoid emergency repairs.
**Q: What are the signs that I need HVAC replacement?**
A: If your system is more than 12–15 years old, needs frequent repairs, or struggles to maintain comfort, replacement may be the best option. Rising utility bills are also a common indicator. A new system often improves comfort and efficiency.
**Q: Do ductless systems work well in larger homes?**
A: Yes, ductless systems can be installed in multiple zones throughout a larger home. They provide precise control for each area, which can save energy. Many homeowners use them alongside central HVAC systems for added flexibility.
**Q: Why is my HVAC unit making strange noises?**
A: Rattling, grinding, or squealing can indicate mechanical issues, loose parts, or airflow problems. Ignoring these noises can lead to bigger repairs. Having a technician check it early helps prevent costly damage.
**Q: Can smart thermostats really save money?**
A: Smart thermostats make it easy to manage heating and cooling schedules. They help reduce wasted energy and can be adjusted remotely from your phone. Many homeowners see noticeable savings on utility bills.
**Q: How does indoor air quality affect my home?**
A: Indoor air can trap dust, mold spores, and allergens that affect your health. Poor air quality often causes discomfort like allergies or breathing issues. Solutions like filters, cleaners, and UV lights can improve the air you breathe daily.
**Q: How long does an HVAC replacement usually take?**
A: Most replacements are completed in a single day. Larger or more complex systems may require more time, but we’ll let you know before starting. Our team works as efficiently as possible to minimize any disruption.
**Q: Do you offer HVAC services for businesses?**
A: Yes, we provide installation, repair, and maintenance for commercial systems of all sizes. Our services help keep your workplace comfortable for staff and customers. Reliable HVAC is an investment in your business.
**Q: What’s included in routine HVAC maintenance?**
A: Maintenance includes cleaning, testing, and inspecting your system for potential problems. We’ll check refrigerant, tighten electrical connections, and replace filters if needed. This keeps your system safe, efficient, and reliable.
