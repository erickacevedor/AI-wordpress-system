## Frequently Asked Questions
Here are some common questions we hear about our HVAC services. If you don’t see yours, get in touch and we’ll provide you with an answer.
**Q: How long does an HVAC installation usually take?**
A: Most installations take about a day, but larger or more complex projects may take longer. Our team will walk you through the timeline before we begin. The focus is always on doing the job right, not just quickly.
**Q: What’s the best way to improve indoor air quality?**
A: Air cleaners, humidifiers, and proper ventilation all play a role. We’ll recommend solutions based on your home’s needs and your health concerns. Cleaner air supports comfort and well-being for your family.
**Q: Do heat pumps work well in cold climates?**
A: Yes, modern heat pumps are built to perform even when temperatures drop. They’re efficient in both heating and cooling, making them a strong year-round option. In extremely low temps, they can work alongside other systems.
**Q: How do I know if my ducts need repair?**
A: Signs include uneven heating or cooling, higher energy bills, and weak airflow from vents. Damaged ducts may also create unusual noises when your system is running. A professional inspection will confirm the problem.
**Q: Should I repair or replace my old HVAC system?**
A: If your unit is over 12 years old and repairs are frequent, replacement is often the smarter choice. A new system saves money on energy and reduces breakdowns. We’ll help you weigh the options based on cost and performance.
**Q: Can ductless systems heat and cool?**
A: Yes, ductless units can handle both heating and cooling, making them versatile year-round. They’re especially helpful for additions or areas where ducts aren’t practical. Many homeowners appreciate the flexibility they provide.
**Q: What happens if I skip regular HVAC maintenance?**
A: Skipping maintenance often leads to breakdowns, higher bills, and shorter system lifespan. Small issues go unnoticed and turn into bigger, costly repairs. Regular service keeps your system running smoothly.
**Q: How do germicidal lights help my system?**
A: Germicidal lights stop mold, bacteria, and other contaminants from spreading inside your HVAC. This improves air quality and helps your system operate more efficiently. It’s a simple installation with long-term benefits.
**Q: What size HVAC system do I need?**
A: The right size depends on your home’s square footage, insulation, and layout. We’ll perform a load calculation to recommend the correct unit. A properly sized system prevents energy waste and uneven comfort.
