## Frequently Asked Questions About Cooling Services
Choosing the right cooling service can feel overwhelming when you are trying to balance comfort, cost, timing, and long-term value. These answers can help you decide whether your home needs AC repair, replacement, or maintenance.
**Q: How do I know if I need AC repair or AC replacement?**
A: Repair may be the better choice if your AC is newer, the problem is minor, and the system has been reliable overall. Replacement may make more sense if the unit is 10 to 15 years old, needs frequent repairs, struggles with humidity, or causes rising energy bills. A professional inspection can help you compare both options before making a decision.
**Q: Why is my AC running but not cooling my home well?**
A: Poor cooling can come from several issues, including a dirty filter, low refrigerant, weak airflow, frozen coils, thermostat problems, duct leaks, or an aging system that can no longer keep up. If your AC still struggles after you replace the filter and check the thermostat, it is best to schedule service before the system works harder than it should.
**Q: Are cooling services worth scheduling before summer?**
A: Yes. Scheduling cooling service before peak summer can help catch worn parts, airflow problems, dirty coils, or performance issues before the hottest days arrive. Waiting until a heat wave can leave you with fewer scheduling options and a higher chance of dealing with an uncomfortable home.
**Q: How often should I schedule AC maintenance?**
A: Most homeowners should schedule AC maintenance once a year, ideally before peak cooling season. If your system runs heavily, serves a larger home, or has a history of repairs, regular maintenance becomes even more important. Consistent service can improve reliability, support efficiency, and help extend equipment life.
**Q: Can better AC service help lower my energy bills?**
A: It can. A clean, properly repaired, well-maintained, or correctly installed AC system usually operates more efficiently than one that is dirty, damaged, oversized, undersized, or neglected. Energy savings depend on your home, ductwork, insulation, thermostat habits, and system condition, but better performance often helps reduce wasted energy.
