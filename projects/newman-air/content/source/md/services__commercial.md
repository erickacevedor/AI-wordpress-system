### When HVAC Trouble Affects Your Business
Heating and cooling problems can disrupt productivity, make customers uncomfortable, and cost you money in energy waste. Because commercial systems are larger and more complex than residential ones, even small issues can have a big impact. Common signs your system needs attention include:
- Uneven temperatures throughout your building
- Higher energy bills without a clear reason
- Strange noises or odors from the HVAC unit
- Frequent breakdowns or inconsistent performance

## Frequently Asked Questions
Here are some of the most common questions about commercial HVAC services. If you don’t see yours, just get in touch and we’ll be glad to answer.
**Q: How often should a commercial HVAC system be serviced?**
A: Most commercial systems should be serviced twice a year; once before the cooling season and once before the heating season. Regular service helps identify issues early and keeps your system running efficiently. Preventive maintenance also reduces downtime and repair costs.
**Q: How do I know if my commercial HVAC system needs replacement?**
A: Signs include rising energy costs, frequent repairs, or a system that’s more than 15 years old. If comfort levels are declining and repairs are becoming routine, replacement may be more cost-effective. A technician can help you weigh the options.
**Q: What’s included in commercial HVAC maintenance?**
A: Maintenance typically includes cleaning coils, checking refrigerant levels, tightening electrical connections, and inspecting filters and belts. These steps improve efficiency and prevent breakdowns. Scheduled maintenance helps extend the life of your system.
**Q: How long does it take to install a commercial HVAC system?**
A: Installation time depends on the size of the building and the type of system being installed. Smaller projects may take a few days, while larger systems can take longer. We’ll provide a clear timeline before work begins.
**Q: Can repairs be done during business hours?**
A: Yes, most repairs can be completed while your business is open. Our technicians work efficiently and with minimal disruption. For larger projects, we’ll coordinate scheduling to fit your needs.
**Q: Will a new commercial HVAC system save money?**
A: Yes, new systems are far more efficient than older ones. The savings in energy costs often offset the investment over time. Improved comfort can also boost productivity and customer satisfaction.
**Q: What’s the difference between commercial and residential HVAC systems?**
A: Commercial systems are larger, more complex, and designed to handle higher demands. They may involve rooftop units, zoning, and advanced controls not typically found in residential setups. Because of these differences, they require specialized service.
**Q: How can I extend the life of my commercial HVAC system?**
A: The best way is through consistent maintenance and timely repairs. Changing filters regularly, scheduling professional inspections, and addressing small issues quickly all help. A well-maintained system can serve your business for many years.
