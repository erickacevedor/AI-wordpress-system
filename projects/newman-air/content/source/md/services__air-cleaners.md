### Why Indoor Air Quality Matters
The air inside your home can hold more pollutants than the air outside, which makes it a hidden problem for many families. Dust, mold spores, pet dander, and even cleaning chemicals circulate through your HVAC system. Over time, this buildup can cause:
- Allergy or asthma flare-ups
- Lingering odors that won’t go away
- Excessive dust on surfaces
- Reduced HVAC efficiency

## Frequently Asked Questions
Here are some common questions about air cleaners. If you don’t see your question here, get in touch and we’ll be happy to provide an answer.
**Q: How does an air cleaner work?**
A: An air cleaner is installed within your HVAC system to filter air as it circulates. It captures particles like dust, pollen, pet dander, and smoke that standard filters often miss. The result is cleaner, healthier air throughout your home.
**Q: Do air cleaners help with odors?**
A: Yes, many air cleaners reduce odors caused by pets, cooking, or smoke. By filtering out particles that hold odors, your air feels fresher and your home smells cleaner. This can make a noticeable difference in everyday comfort.
**Q: What’s the difference between an air cleaner and an air purifier?**
A: Air cleaners generally filter out larger particles like dust and dander, while purifiers may also target bacteria and viruses. Some systems combine both functions. We’ll help you choose the right solution based on your needs.
**Q: How often do air cleaners need maintenance?**
A: Most units require filter changes or cleanings every few months, depending on the system and your home’s air quality. Regular maintenance keeps them working effectively. Our team can guide you on the schedule that works best for your unit.
**Q: Are air cleaners safe for people with allergies or asthma?**
A: Yes, in fact, they’re especially beneficial for people with respiratory issues. By removing allergens and fine particles, air cleaners can reduce symptoms and improve daily comfort. Many families notice improvements soon after installation.
**Q: Will an air cleaner increase my energy bills?**
A: No, in many cases, an air cleaner can improve system efficiency by keeping components cleaner. This helps your HVAC system work more smoothly and may even reduce energy costs over time. The long-term benefits outweigh the small energy use of the unit itself.
**Q: Can I install an air cleaner myself?**
A: Air cleaners are designed to integrate with your HVAC system, so professional installation is strongly recommended. Incorrect installation can reduce performance and even damage your system. Our technicians make sure it’s installed safely and properly.
**Q: How do I know if I need an air cleaner?**
A: Signs include constant dust buildup, lingering odors, allergy symptoms indoors, or visible particles in the air when sunlight comes through windows. Even if your home seems fine, an air cleaner can still improve health and comfort. A professional evaluation can help confirm your needs.
**Q: How long does installation take?**
A: Most air cleaner installations can be completed in a few hours. Our team works efficiently so you can start enjoying cleaner air the same day. We’ll explain the process before beginning so you know exactly what to expect.
