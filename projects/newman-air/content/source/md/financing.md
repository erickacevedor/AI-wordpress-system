## Frequently Asked Questions
If you don’t see your question answered here, reach out, and we’ll be glad to walk you through the details.
**Q: Who do you partner with for financing?**
A: We work with both Synchrony and GoodLeap, two trusted names in home improvement financing. They offer flexible payment plans designed to make HVAC projects more affordable. Our team can help you choose the option that best fits your needs.
**Q: What types of services can I finance?**
A: Most of our services qualify for financing, including AC repair, heating replacements, ductwork projects, indoor air quality upgrades, and full system installations. Whether it’s an urgent repair or a planned project, financing can help cover the cost.
**Q: Is the application process complicated?**
A: No, it’s very simple. Applications are quick, and many customers receive approval the same day. You can apply online or with the help of our team during your appointment.
**Q: Do I need excellent credit to qualify?**
A: Not necessarily. Both Synchrony and GoodLeap consider a range of credit profiles, so you don’t need perfect credit to be approved. Approval depends on your individual credit history.
**Q: Can I reuse my credit line for future projects?**
A: Possibly. If you’re approved, you may be able to use your credit line for additional services in the future. This flexibility is helpful for maintenance and upgrades down the road.
**Q: Are promotional plans offered?**
A: Yes, both Synchrony and GoodLeap provide promotional payment options. These may include low-interest terms, deferred interest, or fixed-term plans that make large projects easier to manage. We’ll review all current offers with you at the time of service.
**Q: Will my payments stay the same each month?**
A: In most cases, yes. Fixed monthly payments are standard with many financing options, making it easier to plan your household budget.
**Q: Does applying affect my credit score?**
A: The application includes a credit check, which may appear on your credit report. Simply requesting more information or asking about your options will not impact your credit score.
**Q: Can financing be combined with other offers?**
A: Often, yes. Financing usually works alongside promotions, rebates, or discounts you may qualify for. We’ll confirm all available savings when we discuss your project.
