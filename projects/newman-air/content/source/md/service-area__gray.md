## Frequently Asked Questions
Here are answers to common questions from homeowners in Gray. If you don’t see your question here, get in touch and we’ll provide you with an answer.
**Q: How often should I service my HVAC system?**
A: It’s recommended to have maintenance done once or twice a year. A spring tune-up helps prepare for the cooling season, while a fall visit gets your heating ready. Regular service helps prevent unexpected breakdowns.
**Q: What are the signs I need a new HVAC system?**
A: Frequent repairs, rising energy bills, and uneven heating or cooling are all signs of a failing system. Units older than 10–15 years often lose efficiency. A new installation can save you money and improve comfort.
**Q: Are ductless HVAC systems efficient?**
A: Yes, ductless systems are known for their energy efficiency and precise temperature control. They avoid energy loss that often happens in ductwork. They’re also great for targeting comfort in specific rooms.
**Q: Why is my HVAC system so loud?**
A: Unusual noises could mean loose components, clogged filters, or mechanical wear. Some sounds are harmless, but others signal the need for repair. A professional inspection will identify the cause.
**Q: Can a smart thermostat help lower energy bills?**
A: Smart thermostats allow you to set schedules and control temperatures remotely. By avoiding wasted heating and cooling, they often reduce utility bills. Many homeowners notice long-term savings.
**Q: How important is indoor air quality?**
A: Poor indoor air can worsen allergies, asthma, and other health issues. Dust, mold, and dander often build up without you noticing. Indoor air solutions improve comfort and support healthier living.
**Q: How long does an HVAC replacement take?**
A: Most replacements are completed in one day. Larger or more complex jobs may take longer, but our team will explain the process upfront. We’ll work efficiently to minimize disruption.
**Q: Do you offer commercial HVAC work?**
A: Yes, our technicians install, repair, and maintain commercial systems. We work with businesses of all sizes in Gray to keep operations comfortable. Reliable HVAC service supports a productive workplace.
**Q: What’s included in HVAC maintenance service?**
A: Maintenance usually involves cleaning, testing, and inspecting your system. We’ll check refrigerant, tighten connections, and replace filters as needed. This routine care keeps your system reliable and efficient.
