## Common Problems with Thermostats
A faulty thermostat can throw your whole system off balance. You may notice uneven heating or cooling, or your system may cycle on and off at the wrong times. Other warning signs include:
- Rooms that don’t reach the temperature you set
- Higher energy bills without increased usage
- Blank screens, unresponsive buttons, or dead batteries
- Constant system cycling or no response from your unit

## Frequently Asked Questions
Here are answers to common questions about thermostat installation and repair. If you don’t see your question here, just reach out and we’ll be glad to help.
**Q: How do I know if my thermostat needs to be replaced?**
A: If your thermostat doesn’t respond, can’t hold the set temperature, or causes your system to cycle constantly, it may be time for a replacement. Sometimes issues are minor, like dead batteries, but repeated problems often signal a failing unit. Our technicians can confirm whether repair or replacement is the better option.
**Q: Can I install a new thermostat myself?**
A: Some basic models can be installed by homeowners, but wiring mistakes or compatibility issues are common. Professional installation guarantees safe setup and proper function with your HVAC system. Plus, we’ll show you how to use it so you get the most benefit.
**Q: What’s the difference between programmable and smart thermostats?**
A: Programmable thermostats let you schedule temperature changes throughout the day, while smart thermostats connect to Wi-Fi and adapt to your habits. Smart models often include mobile control, energy tracking, and integration with home automation systems. Both options can save energy, but smart thermostats offer more flexibility.
**Q: How long does thermostat installation take?**
A: Most installations take about an hour, depending on the model and wiring setup. We’ll remove the old thermostat, connect the new one, and test the system to confirm it’s running smoothly. Before leaving, we’ll walk you through its features.
**Q: Do smart thermostats really save money?**
A: Yes, smart thermostats help lower energy bills by learning your patterns and adjusting heating or cooling automatically. They also allow remote control, so you can avoid wasted energy when you’re away. Over time, many homeowners see noticeable savings.
**Q: Why is my thermostat not turning on my AC or heat?**
A: This could be caused by dead batteries, wiring problems, or a faulty thermostat. Sometimes the issue lies in the HVAC system itself, not the thermostat. Our technicians can run tests to pinpoint the problem.
**Q: How often should I replace my thermostat?**
A: Most thermostats last about 10 years, but you may want to upgrade sooner to take advantage of modern features. If your thermostat is older, it may not communicate effectively with newer HVAC systems. An upgrade can improve both comfort and efficiency.
**Q: Are all thermostats compatible with every HVAC system?**
A: No, compatibility depends on the type of system you have. Our technicians can recommend models that work with your specific equipment and meet your needs. Choosing the right thermostat prevents future performance issues.
**Q: Can thermostat issues affect my whole HVAC system?**
A: Yes, a faulty thermostat can cause your system to run too often, not enough, or at the wrong times. Over time, this can strain your equipment and shorten its lifespan. That’s why quick service is important.
