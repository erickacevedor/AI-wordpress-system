### When Your Air Handler Becomes a Problem
A failing air handler can leave your home uncomfortable and your system working harder than it should. Since it controls airflow, even small issues can quickly affect your heating and cooling. Common signs to watch for include:
- Uneven temperatures in different rooms
- Weak or inconsistent airflow from vents
- Strange noises such as rattling or squealing
- Higher energy bills without a clear cause

## Frequently Asked Questions
Here are some common questions about air handler installation and repair. If you don’t see yours, get in touch and we’ll be glad to help.
**Q: What does an air handler do in an HVAC system?**
A: The air handler is responsible for moving conditioned air throughout your home. It includes components like the blower motor, fan, and filters that keep air circulating. Without it, your HVAC system can’t distribute heating or cooling effectively.
**Q: How do I know if my air handler needs repair?**
A: Signs include weak airflow, loud noises, musty odors, or uneven room temperatures. A sudden spike in energy bills can also point to a problem. If you notice these issues, a technician should check your system.
**Q: How long does an air handler last?**
A: With regular maintenance, most air handlers last 10 to 15 years. Usage, air quality, and maintenance all play a role in lifespan. Replacing older units often improves efficiency and comfort.
**Q: Can an air handler be repaired, or does it need replacement?**
A: Many problems can be repaired, such as faulty motors or clogged filters. If the unit is older or has significant damage, replacement may be more cost-effective. Our team will provide honest recommendations based on your system’s condition.
**Q: Will a new air handler lower my energy bills?**
A: Yes, newer models are designed for efficiency and can reduce energy use. A properly matched air handler also helps your HVAC system perform better overall. The savings often offset the cost over time.
**Q: How often should I schedule maintenance for my air handler?**
A: It’s best to have your air handler serviced at least once a year. Regular maintenance helps prevent breakdowns, improves efficiency, and extends its lifespan. Seasonal tune-ups can keep everything running smoothly.
**Q: Can I replace just the air handler without replacing the entire HVAC system?**
A: In some cases, yes, but compatibility is key. If your HVAC system is older, a full replacement may be recommended for efficiency and performance. A technician can determine the best option for your setup.
**Q: What’s the difference between an air handler and a furnace?**
A: An air handler moves air through your home and is often paired with heat pumps or AC systems. A furnace generates heat by burning fuel and then distributes it through ducts. They serve different purposes, but both are essential for comfort.
**Q: How long does installation or repair take?**
A: Most repairs can be done in a few hours, while installation may take most of the day. The timeline depends on your system and what’s required. We’ll give you an accurate estimate before starting work.
