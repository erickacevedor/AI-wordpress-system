## Frequently Asked Questions
Here are answers to common questions from Elizabethton homeowners. If you don’t see your question here, get in touch and we’ll provide you with an answer.
**Q: How often should my HVAC system be maintained?**
A: Most systems should be serviced once or twice a year. A spring appointment for cooling and a fall appointment for heating will keep your system ready for the season ahead. Regular maintenance helps catch issues early and keep costs down.
**Q: When should I consider replacing my HVAC system?**
A: If your system is more than 10–15 years old, breaks down frequently, or isn’t keeping your home comfortable, it may be time for replacement. A new system can improve efficiency and performance. Our team can help you decide when repair or replacement makes more sense.
**Q: What makes ductless systems a good choice?**
A: Ductless systems provide targeted heating and cooling without the need for ductwork. They’re efficient, quiet, and allow for temperature control in specific rooms. Many homeowners choose them for spaces like additions, garages, or home offices.
**Q: Why is my HVAC system making loud noises?**
A: Noises can indicate loose parts, airflow issues, or worn-out components. Some sounds are harmless, but others can point to serious problems. It’s best to schedule a repair before a small issue leads to a major breakdown.
**Q: Can I save money with a smart thermostat?**
A: Yes, smart thermostats help manage energy use by learning your schedule and adjusting temperatures automatically. They also give you remote control from your phone. Over time, many homeowners see noticeable savings on utility bills.
**Q: Does indoor air quality affect my home?**
A: Indoor air quality can impact health, allergies, and even your sleep. Pollutants like dust, mold, and dander often build up indoors. With the right solutions, you can improve the air you breathe daily.
**Q: How long does an HVAC installation usually take?**
A: Most installations take a day, though larger or more complex systems may take longer. Our technicians will give you a clear timeline before work begins. We’ll always work efficiently while keeping quality a top priority.
**Q: Do you work with commercial HVAC systems?**
A: Yes, our team installs, repairs, and maintains commercial systems in Elizabethton. We handle projects for small offices, retail stores, and larger facilities. Reliable commercial HVAC is key to a comfortable, productive business.
**Q: What’s included in HVAC maintenance?**
A: A typical maintenance service includes cleaning coils, checking refrigerant levels, testing safety controls, and tightening connections. We’ll also inspect for signs of wear and provide recommendations. These visits help extend system life and improve efficiency.
