Frequently Asked Questions

How much do you charge for a diagnostic visit?

Our standard diagnostic fee is $75. A technician will inspect your system, find the issue, and provide a clear, upfront repair estimate before any work starts.

What areas do you service and do you handle emergencies?

We serve homeowners across Washington, Sullivan, Carter, and Greene counties. When your heat fails in winter or your AC breaks in summer, our team responds quickly.

Are your technicians licensed, insured, and certified?

Yes. Newman Air is fully licensed, bonded, and insured in Tennessee. Our 23-person team is background-checked, certified, and professionally trained.

Do you offer financing for new system installations?

Yes. We partner with GoodLeap, Synchrony Bank, and Hearth to offer flexible monthly payment plans with quick online pre-approval.

Should I repair or replace my heating and cooling system?

If your unit is under 10 years old, repair is usually best. If it's over 10 to 12 years old, requires frequent fixes, or drives up electric bills, upgrading to a high-efficiency system saves money over time.
