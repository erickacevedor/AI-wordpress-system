Frequently Asked Questions About Air Conditioning Services

How do I know which AC service I need?

Not cooling, noisy, leaking, or shutting off → start with a repair call. Working but overdue → maintenance or a tune-up. Old or costly to repair → compare repair vs. replacement.

Do I need AC repair if my system still runs?

Possibly — weak airflow, poor humidity control, short cycling, or failing parts can all occur while it runs. If it runs constantly but feels warm or sticky, get it inspected.

Is AC maintenance really worth it in Louisiana?

Yes — long run times through heat and humidity mean maintenance reduces strain, improves airflow, and catches problems before breakdowns.

Can Magnolia Air handle both home and business AC needs?

Yes — residential and commercial services across Dry Prong and surrounding areas.

Should I repair or replace my AC system?

Depends on age, repair history, efficiency, and cost. Newer + minor issue → repair. Older with frequent breakdowns → replacement may be the better value.
