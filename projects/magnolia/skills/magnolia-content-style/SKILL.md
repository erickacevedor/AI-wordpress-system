# Magnolia Air — Content & Writing Style

Write all Magnolia Air copy in the brand's voice: **practical, honest, local, and
low-pressure**. Magnolia Air serves Dry Prong, LA and Central Louisiana (Alexandria,
Pineville, Woodworth, Forest Hill, Ball, Pollock) with AC/HVAC, electrical, and
plumbing — residential and light commercial. NATE-certified technicians.

## Voice in one line

A local expert who helps you pick the *right* service for the problem, explains
options in plain terms, and never pushes you toward the wrong (or bigger) solution.

## Core voice traits

- **Local and specific.** Name the area often: "Dry Prong, LA," "Central Louisiana,"
  and nearby towns. Tie advice to Louisiana heat, humidity, and long cooling seasons.
- **Right-service framing (signature).** The brand's core idea: not every issue needs
  the same fix. Help the reader match the problem to repair vs. maintenance vs.
  tune-up vs. install vs. replacement. "Get the right help without being pushed
  toward the wrong solution."
- **Honest and low-pressure.** Transparent, no upsell. Acknowledge what the homeowner
  can safely check themselves (thermostat, filter, vents) before recommending a call.
- **Plain and clear.** Short sentences. Explain the "why" simply. No hype.
- **Residential *and* commercial.** Call out both audiences and their different
  priorities (home comfort/humidity vs. business uptime/equipment).
- **Trust signals.** NATE-certified technicians, 24/7 emergency service, GoodLeap
  financing / flexible payment, local experience.

## Formatting conventions

- **Headings:** Title Case, descriptive + location, e.g. "Air Conditioning Services
  In Dry Prong, LA", "AC Services That Keep Central Louisiana Comfortable."
- **Sub-headings / card titles:** the service name — "AC Repair Service," "AC
  Maintenance," "Emergency AC Repair."
- **Body:** 1–3 short sentences per paragraph, second person.
- **Lists:** symptom lists and "what to expect" lists work well as emoji-led lines.

## Reusable section patterns

**Hero intro:**
> When your AC struggles in a Dry Prong summer, comfort can disappear fast. Magnolia
> Air provides dependable residential and commercial cooling support across Central
> Louisiana — our NATE-certified technicians help identify the issue, explain your
> options, and recommend the right next step.

**Right-service paragraph (signature):**
> Not every cooling issue needs the same solution. A system blowing warm air may need
> repair, while an older unit with repeated breakdowns may be better suited for
> replacement. Magnolia Air helps you choose the right service based on the problem,
> the system's condition, and the comfort needs of the property.

**When-to-call (self-check then call):**
> You can safely check thermostat settings, replace a dirty filter, and make sure
> vents are open. Beyond that, professional service is the safer, smarter choice.

**Closing CTA:**
> Do not wait until a small cooling issue turns into a costly breakdown. Contact
> Magnolia Air today to schedule your air conditioning service.

## Do / Don't

**Do:** name Dry Prong / Central Louisiana; frame repair-vs-replace honestly; cover
both homeowners and businesses; note NATE-certification, 24/7, and GoodLeap
financing; keep paragraphs short.

**Don't:** hype or superlatives; pushy upsell language; dense technical blocks;
drop the local framing; imply one service fits every problem.
