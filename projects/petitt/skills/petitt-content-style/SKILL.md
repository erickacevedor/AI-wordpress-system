# Petitt Heating & Cooling — Content & Writing Style

Write all Petitt copy in the brand's voice: **local, plain-spoken, and confident
without hype**. Petitt is a family-owned HVAC + plumbing contractor founded in 2010,
based on Homer Worsham Road in Springfield, TN, serving Robertson, Sumner and Davidson
Counties.

## Voice in one line

The neighbor who happens to be a licensed contractor: names your street, tells you what
is actually wrong, quotes the price up front, and shows up when they said they would.

## The four moves that make it sound like Petitt

1. **Locality first.** Name neighborhoods and counties, not just the city: *Tyree
   Springs, Rolling Acres, Walnut Grove, Two Chestnut, Calista, Pleasant Valley, Mims,
   Johnsons Crossroads, Cherry Mound, Goodlettsville, Hendersonville… Robertson and
   Sumner County.* Contrast with national chains: *"We don't just pass through town — we
   live and work here."*
2. **Symptom → reassurance → CTA.** Lead with what the homeowner is feeling ("Don't
   sweat through another humid Tennessee summer"), explain the fix in a sentence, close
   with a **specific** CTA.
3. **Proof in numbers.** Since 2010 · 50+ years combined experience · 1,000+ five-star
   reviews · TN Mechanical & Plumbing licenses · $12/month maintenance plan · $500
   on-time guarantee · 10-year parts-and-labor warranty · 12-hour priority repair.
   Together the last three are **the Petitt Promise** — always name it that.
4. **Seasonal, Middle-Tennessee specifics.** Humid summers, winter freezes, crawl-space
   moisture, hard water, attic heat. Never generic "all seasons" filler.

## Formatting conventions

- **Headings in sentence case** — the theme renders H1–H6 uppercase. Never type
  SHOUTING CAPS into the JSON.
- H1: one per page, includes the service + the city (*"Reliable HVAC & Plumbing Services
  in White House, TN"*).
- H2: benefit- or promise-shaped (*"Year-Round Protection: White House HVAC & Plumbing
  Maintenance Plans"*, *"Why Your Neighbors Trust Petitt Heating & Cooling"*).
- H3: service names in a card grid (*"Cooling & Air Conditioning Services"*).
- Paragraphs: 2–4 sentences. Second person, contractions welcome.
- Bullets: `<strong>Label:</strong> one-sentence explanation.` Keep the label short.
- Use the em dash sparingly; use `&mdash;`, `&amp;`, `&rsquo;` entities in JSON.

## Reusable copy patterns

**Hero intro** — question or symptom → what Petitt does → who it's for:
> "Looking for trusted home comfort experts right down the road? Petitt Heating &
> Cooling delivers fast, dependable heating, cooling, and plumbing services to keep your
> system running smoothly and your family protected all year long."

**Neighborhood line** — the second hero paragraph, listing 8–12 neighborhoods and
closing with *"our licensed technicians are just a quick call away."*

**Trust bar** — three proof points, pipe-separated, one line:
> "⭐ 5-Star Rated Service | Licensed & Insured | Serving White House Since 2010"

**Service card** — H3 service name → one sentence of local framing → 3–4 bullets →
specific CTA (*"Explore White House Cooling Services"*).

**Guarantee bullets** — `<strong>$500 On-Time Guarantee:</strong> We respect your time.
If we don't show up on your scheduled installation day, we pay you $500.`

**FAQ answers** — 2–4 sentences, direct answer in the first sentence, then the local
reason. Mention the Springfield base and same-day reach when it fits.

**Closing CTA** — "We're known for being honest, respectful, and thorough…" → phone
number + schedule button.

## CTA phrasings (use these, not "Learn more")

Call Now · Book Online · Schedule Service · Request Service in White House, TN ·
Explore White House Cooling Services · Sign Up for Our Maintenance Plan · Explore
Financing Options · See Our Full Service Area · Meet the Petitt Family & Team

## SEO copy

- Meta title < 60 chars, service + city + brand:
  *"HVAC & Plumbing Services in White House, TN | Petitt Heating & Cooling"* (trim to fit).
- Meta description < 155 chars, ends with an action.
- Slug: lowercase-hyphenated; service-area pages live at `/service-areas/<city>/`.
- Target keyword in the H1 and the first paragraph — naturally, once.

## Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Our licensed technicians are just a quick call away." | "The best HVAC company in the world." |
| Name the neighborhood | "Serving the greater area" |
| "$12 per month" | "affordable pricing" (unquantified) |
| "the Petitt Promise" | inventing new guarantee names |
| "Petitt Heating & Cooling" on first use, "Petitt" after | "PHC", "Pettit", "Petit" |
| One exclamation mark per page, at most | Exclamation stacks |
