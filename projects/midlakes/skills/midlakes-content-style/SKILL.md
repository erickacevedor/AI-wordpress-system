# Mid Lakes — Content Style

The voice of the prototype at `D:\laragon\www\midlakes\public`. It is **neighbourly
and de-escalating, not promotional.** The reader is a Loganville homeowner whose house
is uncomfortable and who does not know what is wrong. The page's job is to lower the
stakes, explain what might be happening, and make calling feel easy.

> **This is a port.** For the six existing pages the copy is already written — use it
> **verbatim**, entities and all. This skill governs anything *new*: a section the
> brief adds, a rewrite the client asks for, a blog post, a new service page.

## The six rules

1. **Lower the stakes before you sell.** Repeatedly and explicitly tell the reader
   they may not need the expensive thing, or may not need to decide yet:
   *"Sometimes the problem isn't your HVAC equipment at all."*
   *"If a repair makes sense, we'll tell you."*
   *"Financing is simply another option."*
   *"You don't have to know exactly what's wrong before you call us."*
2. **Hedge honestly.** *may*, *can help*, *depends on*, *subject to credit approval*.
   Benefit lists are written as capabilities, not outcomes — *"Encapsulation can
   help:"*, never "will". No promised savings, timelines, or results.
3. **Second person, contractions, short paragraphs.** One to three sentences. Active
   voice. The prototype's paragraphs are genuinely short — match that rhythm.
4. **Local specificity, not local filler.** Loganville first, then Walton and
   Gwinnett Counties. Reach for details a local recognises: Georgia summers, "a hot
   Georgia afternoon", the humidity that "doesn't always stay outside", crawl spaces,
   bonus rooms and additions, older homes without ductwork.
5. **Family-owned is the identity.** It is restated on every page, and it is the
   reason given for everything else — honesty, whole-home thinking, answering the
   phone at 2am.
6. **No hype.** No superlatives, no "state-of-the-art", no competitor jabs, no
   invented statistics, no exclamation marks (the prototype has exactly one, a
   *"Yes!"* opening an FAQ answer).

### ⚠️ The Carrier rule

Write **"a Carrier® dealer"** or **"Carrier® Dealer Quality"**. Do **not** write
*"Carrier Factory Authorized Dealer"* — that is a specific certification and nothing
evidences it. The prototype's copy was corrected to drop "Authorized" for exactly this
reason (`0417f8d`). Restore the word only if the certification is confirmed.
(An earlier draft said Rheem; that was corrected to Carrier across home and
`/service-area/`. Do not reintroduce Rheem.)

Do not claim other brands, awards, review counts, or pricing the brief doesn't supply.
The only prices on the site are the service-agreement rates: **$95 per service visit**,
**+$45 per additional system**, **$0 cancellation penalty**.

## Headline patterns that fit

Sentence case with initial caps on major words. Most carry a **terminal period** on
the home page (*"Join the Mid Lakes Comfort Club."*) and none on the interior pages —
match whichever page you are in.

- Reassurance-first: *"Your Local Team for a More Comfortable Home"*, *"Let's Find the
  Right Way Forward"*, *"Ready to Get Comfortable?"*
- Locality + service: *"HVAC & Home Comfort Services in Loganville, GA"*,
  *"Serving Our Neighbors with Pride Since 2018"*
- The whole-home reframe: *"The Work Most Companies Stop Short Of"*, *"We Look at the
  Whole Home"*
- Plain-value: *"Simple, Transparent Pricing with Zero Commitment"*, *"Built on Doing
  the Right Thing"*
- Reader-voiced FAQ headers: *"What Happens During Your Seasonal Tune-Ups?"*,
  *"Why Loganville Homeowners Consider Financing"*

## Section copy shapes

- **Hero.** H1 = service + city, or the page's subject plainly named. One sub-line
  that is a *reassurance*, not a promise (*"When your home isn't comfortable, you want
  a local team you can trust to find the problem and make it right."*). Two buttons —
  usually `Call (678) 776-0591` and a `#contact` CTA. Optionally a 3–4 item creds row
  of `<strong>value</strong> label` pairs.
- **Eyebrow.** Two or three words, uppercase, naming the section:
  *About Us · Services We Offer · Comfort Club · Why Mid Lakes · Coverage · Pricing ·
  Included · Inspections · Process · Considerations · FAQs · Our story · Our values ·
  Ownership · Community · Credentials · Straight answers*.
- **Lead paragraph.** 2–4 sentences, muted, setting up the section. Multiple `.lead`
  paragraphs stack with a 12–14px gap.
- **Benefit / symptom lists.** Short parallel fragments, **no terminal periods**, 5–8
  items, introduced by a colon phrase (*"We can help with issues such as:"*,
  *"Encapsulation can help:"*, *"It may help you:"*).
- **Detail rows.** H3 naming the service, then 3–6 short paragraphs that go
  *symptom → what we do → how we explain it → what you decide*, then one CTA whose
  label names the specific action (*"Schedule Thermostat Service"*, *"Request a Crawl
  Space Estimate"*).
- **Process steps.** Title is a plain imperative or a decision (*"Start With an
  Evaluation"*, *"Decide Whether You'd Like to Finance"*) + two short paragraphs.
- **FAQ.** The question exactly as a homeowner would type it. The answer opens with
  the honest short answer — *"Yes."*, *"Absolutely."*, *"No."*, *"Not at all."* — then
  the conditions.
- **Closing contact band.** H2 as an invitation, 1–4 short `contact-sub` paragraphs
  that de-escalate, then the phone number spelled out in prose (*"Call (678) 776-0591
  or schedule your service online today."*) plus a CTA button.

## Mechanics

- **Company name.** `Mid Lakes Heating & Cooling, LLC` in the footer and legal;
  **`Mid Lakes HVAC`** in most body copy; `Mid Lakes` alone once the section has
  established it. Never "MidLakes" or "Mid-Lakes".
- **Phone.** `(678) 776-0591` → `tel:+16787760591`. It appears in the header nav, the
  hero buttons, the contact `dl`, the footer, and inline in prose.
- **Address.** `2834 Horseshoe Rd, Loganville, GA 30052`.
- **Hours.** `Monday–Friday: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM` (en dashes), with
  `After-hours service available 24/7` as the sub-line.
- **CTA labels.** Action + object, title case, no trailing punctuation —
  *Get a Free Estimate · Call Now: (678) 776-0591 · Schedule Service ·
  Request Service · Sign Up for a Service Agreement · Join the Mid Lakes Comfort Club ·
  Explore Financing Options · See Our Service Area · Submit Request*.
- **Entities.** "air conditioner" / "AC" (not "A/C"), "ductless mini-split"
  (hyphenated), "mini-split", "ductwork", "heat pump", "tune-up", "crawl space"
  (two words), "encapsulation", "whole-home", "Comfort Club", "Service Agreement".
- **Punctuation in HTML settings.** `&rsquo;` for apostrophes, `&mdash;` for em dashes
  (unspaced, as the prototype writes them), `&ndash;` for ranges, `&amp;` for
  ampersands, `&reg;` after Carrier. Keep JSON UTF-8.
- **Internal links.** Root-relative, descriptive anchors: `/about-us/`, `/services/`,
  `/service-agreements/`, `/service-area/`, `/financing/`, `/blog/`. The contact
  target is the on-page anchor **`#contact`** — there is no `/contact` page.
- **Both financing CTAs point at `#contact`** on purpose. That is a settled decision,
  not a placeholder. Do not "fix" it to a lender URL.

## Anti-patterns — do not write these

- *"Experience the unbeatable, state-of-the-art comfort solutions…"* — brochure voice.
- *"We'll have it fixed before you know it."* — unearned promises.
- *"Unlike the big franchises who…"* — the prototype makes the local point by
  **stating what it is** (*"we aren't a big regional franchise—we're your local
  neighbors"*), never by attacking a competitor by name or type twice over.
- *"Carrier Factory Authorized Dealer"* — see the Carrier rule.
- Invented review counts, "hundreds of 5-star reviews", awards, or years of experience
  attributed to an individual technician.
- Stacking exclamation marks or emoji into body copy. Emoji are decorative icons in
  this design, not punctuation.
