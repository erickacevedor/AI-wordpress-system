Ductless / Mini-Split Systems

Some rooms are simply harder to keep comfortable than others. A home addition, bonus room, garage, workshop, or older property without existing ductwork may need a different approach.
Ductless mini-splits provide flexible, efficient heating and cooling without requiring a traditional duct system. Mid Lakes HVAC provides complete ductless installation, repair, and maintenance services to help you get dependable comfort where you need it.
Ductless / Mini-Split Installation. Every home is different, so we don’t believe in a one-size-fits-all approach. Our team can help determine the right system size, number of indoor units, and configuration based on your space and comfort goals.
Ductless / Mini-Split Repair. If your mini-split isn’t heating or cooling properly, starts leaking, makes unusual noises, or stops responding, we’ll diagnose the issue and explain your repair options clearly.
Ductless / Mini-Split Maintenance. Routine maintenance helps keep your ductless equipment clean, efficient, and ready for the seasons ahead. It can also help catch small issues before they turn into larger repairs.
Whether you’re considering your first mini-split or need help with a system already in your home, our team is ready to help.

Whole-Home Comfort & Efficiency

Sometimes the problem isn’t your HVAC equipment at all.
Poor insulation, excess humidity, or moisture beneath your home can make your heating and cooling system work harder and leave certain rooms feeling uncomfortable. That’s why Mid Lakes HVAC looks beyond the equipment itself when your home isn’t feeling the way it should.

Crawl Space Encapsulation

What happens beneath your home can affect the comfort above it.
When ground moisture and humid outdoor air enter an unprotected crawl space, they can contribute to high humidity, musty odors, mold-friendly conditions, and deterioration of materials beneath the home.
Crawl space encapsulation helps separate the area from outside moisture and creates a cleaner, more controlled environment.
Our team can take a look at your crawl space, explain what we’re seeing, and recommend an approach that makes sense for your property.
Request a Crawl Space Estimate

  • ✓ Control humidity beneath the home
  • ✓ Reduce conditions that encourage mold growth
  • ✓ Help protect structural materials
  • ✓ Minimize musty odors
  • ✓ Support more comfortable indoor conditions
  • ✓ Improve the overall efficiency of the home

Attic Insulation

If your home is difficult to keep comfortable, your attic may be part of the problem.
Inadequate insulation allows heat to move more easily between the attic and your living space. During Georgia summers, that can mean more heat entering the home. In colder weather, it can allow the warmth you’re paying for to escape.
Mid Lakes HVAC provides attic insulation upgrades designed to help your home hold onto conditioned air and make life a little easier on your HVAC system.
If certain rooms are always too hot or too cold, or your system seems to run more than it should, we can help determine whether your attic insulation may be contributing to the problem.

  • ✓ Reduce summer heat gain
  • ✓ Reduce winter heat loss
  • ✓ Maintain more consistent indoor temperatures
  • ✓ Keep conditioned air inside the living space
  • ✓ Reduce unnecessary HVAC workload
  • ✓ Support improved energy efficiency

Humidity Control & Air Quality

Anyone who lives in Georgia knows the humidity doesn’t always stay outside.
When moisture levels inside your home get too high, the air can feel sticky or clammy even when the thermostat says the temperature should be comfortable. Excess humidity can also contribute to condensation, musty odors, dust mites, and conditions that support mold growth.
Mid Lakes HVAC provides humidity-control solutions designed to help your home feel more balanced and comfortable.
We’ll listen to what you’ve been noticing in your home, evaluate the conditions, and recommend a solution that works with your existing comfort system.
