Air Purification FAQs

What does a whole-home air purifier do?

A whole-home air purifier works with your HVAC system to help reduce particles and odors as air circulates through the home. Depending on the system, it may help with dust, pollen, pet dander, stale air, and other common indoor air concerns.

Is a whole-home air purifier better than a portable air purifier?

A portable air purifier only treats the room where it is placed. A whole-home air purifier works with your HVAC system, which allows it to support cleaner air across more of the home.

How do I know if I need air purification?

You may benefit from air purification if dust returns quickly, odors linger, pet dander is a concern, pollen collects indoors, or standard filters do not seem to keep up.

Can air purification help with allergies?

Air purification may help reduce particles like pollen, dust, and pet dander that can contribute to irritation for some households. It is not a medical treatment, but it can be part of a cleaner indoor air strategy.

Do I need professional installation for a whole-home air purifier?

Yes. Whole-home air purifier installation should be handled by an HVAC professional to make sure the system is compatible, placed correctly, and supported by proper airflow. FAQ

Humidity Control FAQs

How do I know if my home has too much humidity?

Common signs include sticky air, musty odors, damp rooms, condensation on windows, allergy-like symptoms indoors, and a home that feels uncomfortable even when the AC is running. Musty odors are often connected to excess moisture, so they should not be ignored.

What indoor humidity level is best?

Many homes feel most comfortable when indoor humidity stays around 30% to 50%. The right range can depend on the season, home conditions, and comfort needs.

Can my AC remove humidity?

Yes, your AC removes some moisture as it cools. However, if the system is short cycling, oversized, dirty, or struggling with airflow, it may not remove enough humidity to keep your home comfortable.

Is a whole-home dehumidifier better than a portable dehumidifier?

A whole-home dehumidifier can manage moisture more broadly through the HVAC system, while portable units only treat specific rooms. The best option depends on your home, humidity levels, and comfort goals.

Do I need humidity control if my home already has central AC?

Possibly. Central AC can help with moisture, but some homes still struggle with humidity because of airflow issues, system sizing, ductwork, basement moisture, or long humid summers.
