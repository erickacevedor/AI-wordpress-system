# Dolan Design HVAC — Content & Writing Style

Write all Dolan copy in the brand's voice: **local, family-owned, plain-English, and
no-pressure**. Dolan Design HVAC & Plumbing is a Louisburg, NC company serving Franklin
& Wake County — HVAC *and* residential plumbing, 25+ years, family-owned.

## Voice in one line

A neighbor who's fixed it all before, explains what went wrong in plain English,
gives you an honest price up front, and never hard-sells.

## Core voice traits

- **Local and specific.** Name the area often: Louisburg, Raleigh, Wake Forest,
  Youngsville, Zebulon, Franklinton, "Franklin & Wake counties," "Central North
  Carolina." Tie advice to NC heat, humidity, and local building codes.
- **Family-owned, been-here-forever.** "We live here, too." 25+ years, "we're right
  down the road and ready to help."
- **Plain English, no corporate pitch.** "You don't need a corporate sales pitch — you
  need cold air, fast." Explain the real issue simply.
- **Honest, upfront pricing.** No high-pressure tactics, no unnecessary replacements,
  no hidden fees. Flexible **0% financing**.
- **Dual-trade edge.** Licensed in both HVAC and plumbing — understands how the home's
  systems interact.
- **Trust signals.** 25+ years, 24/7 emergency service, honest recommendations, all
  major brands, financing. Tagline: **"Better Air. Better Life."**

## Formatting conventions

- **Headings:** Title Case, descriptive + location — "Professional Cooling & AC
  Services in Louisburg, NC", "Why Franklin & Wake County Neighbors Choose Dolan."
- **Sub-headings / card titles:** the service — "Emergency AC Repair," "Ductless
  Mini-Split Systems."
- **Body:** 1–3 short sentences, second person.

## Reusable section patterns

**Hero intro:**
> North Carolina summers are brutal, and when the humidity hits Franklin and Wake
> counties, you need an AC you can count on. As a local, family-owned team with over
> 25 years of experience, we've fixed just about every cooling disaster imaginable —
> so we're right down the road and ready to help.

**Service blurb (plain + reassuring):**
> When your AC cuts out in the middle of a heatwave, you don't need a corporate sales
> pitch — you need cold air, fast. We diagnose the real mechanical issue, explain what
> went wrong in plain English, and fix it right the first time without surprise fees.

**Why-choose bullets:** 25+ years local experience · dual-trade HVAC + plumbing
expertise · honest upfront pricing · flexible 0% financing.

**Closing CTA:**
> Don't spend another day sweating inside your own living room. Call Dolan Design today
> at (919) 896-8630 or send us a message online to get your service scheduled!

## Do / Don't

**Do:** name Louisburg / Franklin & Wake County; lead with plain-English honesty;
note 25+ years, dual-trade, upfront pricing, 0% financing; keep paragraphs short; end
with a clear CTA.

**Don't:** hype or superlatives; pushy upsell; dense technical blocks; drop the local
family-owned framing.

## Company name usage

**Dolan Design HVAC & Plumbing** in body; **Dolan Design** acceptable in labels.
Consistent within a page.
