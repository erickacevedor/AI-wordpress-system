Gas Furnaces (Natural Gas & Propane)

A gas furnace delivers fast, toasty heat through your vents. Because we are licensed in both HVAC and plumbing, we safely service gas valves, burner assemblies, ignitors, flame sensors, and exhaust flues to keep your home warm and safe from carbon monoxide leaks.

Dual-Fuel (Hybrid) Heating Systems

A dual-fuel system pairs an electric heat pump with a gas furnace backup. It uses the heat pump on mild 70° days and switches over to gas heat during winter freezes. We install and service hybrid systems to give you the lowest possible monthly heating bill.

Ductless Mini-Split Systems

Do you have a garage that feels like a walk-in freezer, or a bonus room that makes you immediately grab a blanket? A mini-split gives you fast, quiet heat in those chilly rooms, without messing with the rest of the house or starting a thermostat argument.

Is Your Heater Acting Up? 5 Signs It’s Time to Give Us a Call

Most heating problems give you a few signs before the entire system stops working. If you notice any of these issues, giving us a call now can prevent an expensive emergency breakdown later:

🥶 Cool or lukewarm air — rooms never reach the temperature you set.

🔊 Loud sounds — screeching, banging, persistent clicking, or rattling while it runs.

👃 Odd smells — burning plastic, sulfur, or a heavy damp smell that lingers in the air.

⏱️ Short-cycling — the system shuts off after only two or three minutes.

💸 A power bill that spikes out of nowhere, without a big change in the weather.

Don’t wait for a total breakdown on the coldest night of the year. Let our team take a look and fix the small stuff before it becomes a big problem.

Frequently Asked Questions About Home Heating

How do I know if I have a heat pump or a furnace?

Look at your outdoor unit in the winter. If it runs when you turn on the heat, you have a heat pump. If it only turns on in the summer for AC and your indoor heater runs on gas, you have a furnace.

How much does heating repair or replacement cost with Dolan Design?

Repairs start with a simple, flat diagnostic fee to find the problem. Replacements depend on your home’s size, but we always give you an exact, upfront price with zero hidden fees before we touch a thing.

How often should my heating system be serviced?

Once a year in the early fall months. A quick pre-winter tune-up catches worn parts before they break, keeps your power bills low, and protects your warranty.

Is a heat pump better than a gas furnace in North Carolina?

It depends on your setup. Heat pumps are usually cheaper to run in our mild NC winters, but gas furnaces give you faster, warmer heat on super cold nights. Many local homeowners actually use both in a hybrid system.

What does it mean when &ldquo;Aux Heat&rdquo; turns on?

It means your heat pump called in backup power, like electric heating strips, because it’s freezing outside or you raised the thermostat by several degrees. If it stays on during mild weather above 45°F, give us a call.

How long does a typical home heating system last?

Heat pumps usually last 12 to 15 years because they work year-round for both heating and cooling. Standalone gas furnaces can last 15 to 20 years since they only run a few months out of the year.
