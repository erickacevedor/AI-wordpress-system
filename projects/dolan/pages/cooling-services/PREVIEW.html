Frequently Asked Questions

What HVAC brands does Dolan Design service and install?

All major cooling brands—Trane, Mitsubishi Electric, Daikin, Carrier, Lennox, Rheem, and Goodman—using factory-matched parts.

What happens when I call for an AC quote or service?

You speak directly with experienced technicians, get a straightforward diagnostic, and transparent flat-rate pricing with no hidden fees.

Should I repair or replace my air conditioner?

Replace if it’s 10–12+ years old, needs frequent repairs, or a single repair costs more than 50% of a new system.

Why is my AC blowing warm air all of a sudden?

Usually a clogged filter, tripped breaker, frozen coil, or refrigerant leak. Turn off the thermostat and call a licensed technician.

How often should I change my air filter in North Carolina?

Every 30–60 days—monthly during peak summer heat and pollen season.
